What does FemFactory do?

FemFactory is a freelance marketing agency that connects brands with the right talents to build tailored marketing teams.

Which services are available?

360 campaigns, paid ads, strategy, branding and design, social media, copywriting in Arabic and English, video production and more. See the services section above.

Where is the team based?

FemFactory is based in Cairo, Egypt, and was established in 2025.

How do I start a project?

Send a brief through FemFactory's profile on OTJ (One Time Job). The team answers a few questions with you before booking.
